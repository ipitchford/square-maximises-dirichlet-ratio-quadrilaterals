# Status

This is an **unrefereed candidate**, version `0.1.0-candidate`, dated 2026-10-03. The creator is Anonymous.

**Claimed.**
- Theorem 1: for every simple quadrilateral $Q$, convex or not, $\lambda_2(Q)/\lambda_1(Q)\le5/2$ for the Dirichlet Laplacian, with
  equality only for squares.
- The intermediate results stated in the paper: Lemmas A and B; Theorem 2 (local step, $0<\lvert p\rvert_\infty\le1/20$); Theorem 3
  ($0<\lvert p\rvert_\infty\le0.225$); the transport cover; Lemma $B'$; Theorem $1'$ (convex case); Theorem 5 (non-convex case) with the
  lemmas of Supplement S.
- The exact first-order calculation of §1: for the isosceles trapezoid direction the true first-order term vanishes. Rule (3.16) of
  Arbon (2022) instead gives $-\sqrt{16384+729\pi^4}/(18\pi^2)\approx-1.664$ per unit $t$.

**Not claimed.**
- The supremum of the ratio over darts. The value $7/3$ in §5.1 is a numerical observation, not proved and not used.
- A quantitative stability statement near the square.
- Formal verification; independent reproduction by an unaffiliated party; specialist or editorial peer review.
- Historical priority beyond the bounded search ([PRIOR_ART.md](PRIOR_ART.md)).

**Computations used by the proof.** Every certified cover was replayed from its final log by its fail-closed verifier. The archive
as frozen was replayed from a fresh extraction: [REPLAY_RECEIPT.md](REPLAY_RECEIPT.md).

**Reviews.**
- During development, each part was reviewed adversarially by a second AI model (OpenAI), prompted to refute it. Findings and repairs
  are in paper Appendix C.
- An external referee report of 2 October 2026, supplied by the publisher, recommended major revisions. All items were addressed;
  see [REFEREE_RESPONSE.md](REFEREE_RESPONSE.md). The reviewer's identity is not recorded, and the report's text is not
  redistributed.
- None of this is specialist or journal peer review.
